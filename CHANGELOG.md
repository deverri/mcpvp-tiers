## MCPvP Tiers 1.0.0 (Minecraft 26.3)

First release of MCPvP Tiers, an unofficial fork of Tiers 0.9.1 by Flavio6561.

- MCPvP tiers on nametags, the tab list and chat, covering all 16 MCPvP kits with MCPvP's tier colours.
- The player lookup now shows MCTiers, PvPTiers, Subtiers and MCPvP side by side.
- MCPvP column in the config screen. Cycling and auto kit detect also cover MCPvP kits.
- Fixed gamemode cycling skipping modes for Subtiers.
- Settings now live in `config/MCPvPTiers.json`. Remove the original Tiers mod before installing.
